For retailers & CSR partners

A supply chain that's also a story worth telling.

Every punnet on the shelf traces back to a named household, not an anonymous farm. That's the difference between a supply agreement and a CSR campaign your customers actually notice.

What retailers get

  • A consistent, graded supply of oyster mushrooms at an agreed wholesale rate
  • A genuine community-development story to attach to shelf and campaign marketing
  • Co-branding opportunities across grower agreements, collection points, and packaging
  • Traceability back to the household or community group that grew each batch

What CSR partners fund

  • Subsidising spawn cost alongside the Agriculture Office allocation
  • Grow media kits for households on the deposit tier
  • Collection point logistics in new communities
  • Measurable, reportable impact: households onboarded, kilograms bought back, income generated
