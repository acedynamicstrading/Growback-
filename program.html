Program mechanics

From free spawn to a retail shelf, in five steps.

01

Sign up & sign

Register through the grower portal and sign the buyback & no-side-selling agreement.

02

Receive spawn

Collect free spawn, and grow media too if you've chosen the deposit tier.

03

Grow

6–8 weeks of fruiting, with support materials provided through the portal.

04

Harvest & sell back

Bring the harvest in at collection points; it's weighed and paid at the fixed program price.

05

Repeat, or return media

Start a new batch, or return the media once your agreed cycles are done to get your deposit back.

Two tiers, one agreement

Choose based on what you already have

A Tier A

Spawn only

  • Cost: Free
  • Deposit: None
  • Requires: Your own bag, bucket, or container
  • Commitment: Sell harvest back at the fixed price; no side-selling
B Tier B

Spawn + grow media kit

  • Cost: Free spawn, kit provided
  • Deposit: Refundable — held on the media, not the spawn
  • Deposit returned: After an agreed number of successful buyback cycles, or on return of the media in good condition
  • Commitment: Sell harvest back at the fixed price; no side-selling

The agreement

What every grower signs

Signed digitally in the grower portal before spawn is issued. Full legal language to be finalised; summary below.

1. Exclusivity of sale

The grower agrees to sell 100% of harvest from program spawn back to GrowBack at the published buyback price. Selling to any other buyer is a breach of this agreement.

2. Grow media (Tier B only)

Media supplied under Tier B remains the property of GrowBack until the deposit is returned. The grower is responsible for reasonable care of the media between cycles.

3. Quality standard

Harvest is graded at collection. Contaminated, spoiled, or undersized produce may be rejected or paid at a reduced rate, per the published grading standard.

4. Deposit return

Deposits are returned after the agreed number of buyback cycles are completed, or on return of the media in acceptable condition, whichever the grower chooses at sign-up.

5. Exit

A grower may leave the program at any time by returning any outstanding media; deposits are settled according to condition and cycles completed at that point.

FAQ

Common questions

What if my batch fails?

Contamination and low yields happen. Spawn replacement policy and any deposit implications will be set out in the full agreement before launch.

Can I sell some outside the program?

No — the agreement requires the full harvest from program spawn to be sold back to GrowBack. This is what makes the guaranteed buyback and retail pipeline possible.

Do I need experience?

No. Growing guides and support are provided through the portal after sign-up.

How often can I collect spawn?

Cadence will be set with the Agriculture Office based on allocation — details to follow at pilot launch.
