Join as a grower
A community cultivation program

We give the spawn. We buy back the harvest.

GrowBack partners with the Agriculture Office to put free oyster mushroom spawn into households, guarantees a buyback price for what they grow, and routes the produce to local shops and supermarkets through CSR-backed retail partnerships.

Register as a grower See how it works
The cycle

How the buyback loop works

100% of harvest grown on program spawn is guaranteed a buyer, at a fixed price, for every registered grower — no exceptions.
Two ways in

Every grower chooses their level of commitment

Spawn alone is free. If a household also needs the grow media — a bag, bucket, or container — we provide it against a refundable deposit rather than a purchase, so the barrier to starting stays low.

A Tier A

Spawn only

For households who already have a bag, bucket, or shaded space set up.

  • Free spawn from the AG office allocation
  • No deposit required
  • Sign the buyback & no-side-selling agreement
  • Guaranteed buyback at the fixed program price
B Tier B

Spawn + grow media kit

For households starting from nothing.

  • Free spawn, plus a grow bag, bucket, or container
  • Refundable deposit held against the media, returned after an agreed number of buyback cycles
  • Sign the buyback & no-side-selling agreement
  • Guaranteed buyback at the fixed program price

Buying media for your own use?

You don't need to join the program to grow at home. Grow bags, buckets, containers, and starter spawn are available to purchase directly, no agreement required.
