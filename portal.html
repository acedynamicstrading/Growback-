Growing guide

General guidance for oyster mushroom cultivation on program spawn. Crop-specific and export-grade guides will expand here as the program grows into other crops and media.

Spawning & incubation (Days 1–14)

Keep the bag or bucket in a dark, well-ventilated space away from direct sun. Room temperature (24–28°C) is ideal. You'll see white mycelium spreading through the substrate — this is normal and should fully colonise within 2 weeks.

Fruiting (Days 14–21)

Once colonised, move to a slightly humid, indirectly lit spot. Mist lightly twice a day. Small pins will appear within a few days — resist the urge to open the bag too early.

Harvesting

Harvest when caps flatten but before edges curl upward. Twist gently at the base rather than cutting, to avoid leaving stubs that can rot. Expect 2–3 flushes per bag, roughly a week apart.

Common problems

Green or black patches: likely contamination — isolate the bag away from others and report it so we can track contamination rates. No pins forming: usually too little humidity or light — increase misting and indirect light. Slow growth: check the space isn't too cold.

Report an issue

Contamination, pests, or anything going wrong with your batch — log it here so it's tracked against your record.
